## Summary

The codebase is small (about 8,400 lines across ten crates), builds clean with no clippy warnings, and the architectural rule that simulation crates never import rendering or UI holds everywhere. The problems concentrate in three places.

First, **the simulation runs different physics in headless mode than in the GUI.** The spatial hash is rebuilt once per frame in `PreUpdate` while movement happens in `FixedUpdate`, so any frame that runs several fixed ticks (headless at `--speed 10`, or the GUI at high speed) has predation, disease, symbiosis and mate search reading stale positions. The GUI speed control also changes the fixed timestep rate, which shrinks the delta every virtual-time timer sees, while headless scales virtual time and leaves the timers alone. Every tuning conclusion drawn from headless sweeps since April carries this caveat.

Second, **a handful of small arithmetic and accounting defects in `clauvolution_sim` and `clauvolution_world` plausibly bias the dynamics the project exists to watch.** The largest is the moisture map being normalised to −1..1 while the biome thresholds and the vegetation carrying-capacity formula assume 0..1: half the land can never regrow vegetation. Smaller ones: the volcano only strikes one quadrant, small organisms mint energy on reproduction, two attackers can both be paid for one kill, and event deaths never reach the per-cause totals.

Third, **documentation had drifted from the code** in about ten places (flags, controls, a tuning constant, a design decision describing an approach that was not the one shipped, two roadmap items still listed as future though shipped in April). All of those were small markdown edits and were fixed on this branch under the fix-it-now rule; they appear in the closed list.

The genome and brain crates have several design choices worth knowing about (locally numbered hidden neurons, an unbounded morphology term in the compatibility distance, recurrent edges that silently read zero) that are recorded as `Design` findings and left as inventory. They are not bugs in the sense of doing something other than intended, but they shape what evolution can find, and anyone tuning speciation should read them first.

## Invariants

## Findings

### Bug

- `moisture-range-mismatch` — **Moisture is normalised to −1..1 but biome thresholds and vegetation capacity assume 0..1.** Bug · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_world/src/lib.rs:210-216` (`generate_noise_map`, normalisation); `:83-97` (`Tile::from_elevation_moisture`, thresholds 0.25 and 0.6); `:223-232` (`tile_dynamics_system`, `capacity = nutrients * moisture`).
  - Both noise maps are rescaled to −1..1. Elevation wants that (water is negative). Moisture does not: `from_elevation_moisture` classifies anything under 0.25 as Sand or Rock and only above 0.6 as Forest, so Forest is confined to the top fifth of the range, and `tile_dynamics_system` computes a negative carrying capacity on roughly half the land, driving vegetation to zero with no regrowth. Food regeneration accepts spawns with probability `(vegetation + nutrients)/2`, so those tiles also feed foragers less. Suggested fix: rescale moisture to 0..1 (or map `(m + 1) / 2` at the call site) and re-run the attractor audit; this is a candidate contributor to the green-world attractor in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
  - Review backlog: `moisture-range-normalisation`

- `frozen-total-organisms-stat` — **`SimStats.total_organisms` is set at startup and load and never updated.** Bug · Open · Verified.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_app/src/main.rs:290` and `:260` (only writes); `crates/clauvolution_ui/src/lib.rs:237` (`header_bar_system`, "Pop:" label); `crates/clauvolution_app/src/main.rs:632` (`print_headless_summary`).

The header bar's Pop readout shows the same frozen number. `total_food` on the same struct is never written at all. The Graphs tab is correct because `record_population_history` recounts. Suggested fix: drop the two fields and read counts from the latest `PopulationHistory` snapshot, or recount in `record_population_history` and write them there.
  - Review backlog: `sim-accounting-fixes`

- `convergence-chronicle-spam` — **The convergent-evolution chronicle entry is re-logged on every classification pass because its dedupe string does not match its own text.** Bug · Open · Verified.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:1307-1316` (`species_classification_system`).

- `volcano-lower-left-quadrant` — **The volcano centre is rolled in 0..256 on a 512×512 world.** Bug · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:308-309` (`mass_extinction_input_system`).
  - `rng.gen_range(0.0..256.0)` for both axes while `config.world_width`/`world_height` are 512, so the eruption can never land in three quarters of the world. Nutrient rain, two branches later, uses the config values correctly. Fix: use `config.world_width as f32` and `world_height`.
  - Review backlog: `volcano-full-world-range`

- `reproduction-child-energy-mint` — **Small parents pay less for reproduction than the child receives.** Bug · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:1075-1078` (`reproduction_system`, cost `40 × (0.5 + size × 0.5)`); `:1131` (child `Energy(config.reproduction_energy_cost * 0.8)`, a fixed 32).
  - At `body_size` 0.5 the parent pays 30 and the child is born with 32, so each birth creates 2 energy from nothing; at the 0.3 floor the surplus is 6. Above `body_size` 1.0 the sign flips and the transaction becomes a tax. This is a gradient toward small bodies that is not part of any documented decision (`DECISIONS.md` attributes the small-body drift to plant dominance). Fix: give the child a fixed fraction of what the parent actually paid.
  - Review backlog: `sim-accounting-fixes`

- `multi-attacker-kill-duplication` — **Two attackers can both be paid for the same victim in one tick, and the kill is counted twice.** Bug · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:609-661` (`predation_system`).
  - `kills` is a flat `Vec<(killer, victim, energy)>`. Each attacker breaks after its first successful target, but nothing stops several attackers picking the same victim, so each receives `victim_energy × 0.1` of the victim's unmodified energy, and `predation_stats.kills += kills.len()`. Supporting evidence, not a clean reproduction: the baseline summary shows 4724 kills against 1946 predation deaths. Fix: dedupe by victim before applying (first registered attacker wins, or split the transfer).
  - Review backlog: `sim-accounting-fixes`

### Design

- `spatial-hash-rebuilt-per-frame` — **The spatial hash is rebuilt once per frame in `PreUpdate` while positions change once per fixed tick.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_world/src/lib.rs:11` (`WorldPlugin::build`); `:285-296` (`update_spatial_hash`); readers in `clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs`: `sensing_and_brain_system`, `predation_system:612`, `disease_transmission_system`, `symbiosis_tracking_system`, `reproduction_system:1084`.
  - Whenever a frame runs more than one `FixedUpdate` tick, every tick after the first queries neighbours at positions from the start of the frame. That is every frame in headless at the default `--speed 10`, and any GUI frame at speed above 1× or after a hitch. Entities that moved out of range are still returned; entities that moved into range are missed; newly spawned organisms are invisible to neighbours until the next frame. The GUI at 1× runs roughly one tick per frame and is barely affected, so headless sweeps and the watched sim are not measuring the same world. Fix: move `update_spatial_hash` into the `FixedUpdate` chain immediately after `tick_counter_system`, and drop the `PreUpdate` registration. Invariants 2 and 3.
  - Review backlog: `spatial-hash-fixed-tick`

- `gui-speed-rescales-timers` — **The GUI speed control changes the fixed timestep rate, which shrinks every virtual-time timer's delta; headless scales virtual time and leaves them alone.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:229` (`sim_speed_system`, `set_timestep_hz(30 × multiplier)`); `crates/clauvolution_app/src/main.rs:545` (`set_headless_speed`, `set_relative_speed`).
  - At GUI speed 16× the fixed clock runs at 480 Hz and `Res<Time>` inside `FixedUpdate` reports a 1/480 s delta, so the 5 s species-classification timer and the 1 s history sampler need 16× more ticks to fire, and "sim time" in the header advances 16× slower per tick than in headless. The comment at `main.rs:477-483` says timer semantics are preserved; that is true only of the headless path. Fix: have the GUI also use `Time<Virtual>::set_relative_speed` and leave the fixed rate at 30 Hz, which is the approach `DECISIONS.md` already records for headless. Invariant 3.
  - Review backlog: `headless-gui-parity`

- `species-threshold-flag-gui-ignored` — **`--species-threshold` is parsed for both modes but only applied in headless.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_app/src/main.rs:113-122` (parsed, passed only to `run_headless`); `:549-559` (`HeadlessSpeciesThreshold`, `apply_species_threshold`, headless startup chain only).
  - A GUI run with the flag silently uses the default 1.3. Either apply it in both startup chains or reject it in GUI mode. Invariant 3.
  - Review backlog: `headless-gui-parity`

- `mate-energy-unscaled-and-unpaid` — **The mate's eligibility uses the unscaled threshold and the mate pays nothing.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:1092-1096` (`reproduction_system`).
  - The initiator must exceed `threshold × (0.5 + size × 0.5)` and pays a size-scaled cost; the mate only needs `energy > config.reproduction_energy_threshold` and, because `mate_candidates` is a collected snapshot of values, cannot be debited. Sexual reproduction is therefore half price compared with asexual, which may be intended (a bonus for finding a mate) but is not recorded in `DECISIONS.md`. Decide and document, or charge the mate a share.

- `hidden-neuron-ids-not-innovated` — **New hidden neuron ids are `max(local ids) + 1`, not drawn from the innovation counter.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_genome/src/lib.rs:543` (`mutate_add_neuron`); `:607-616` (`crossover`, neuron graft by id).
  - Two lineages that each split a connection get a neuron with the same id that means different things. Crossover pulls missing neurons from the other parent by id, so a connection can be grafted onto a semantically unrelated neuron, silently. Evaluation is unaffected because it only needs ids to be locally unique, and `compatibility_distance` uses connection innovations, not neuron ids, so speciation is unaffected too. Classic NEAT keeps a global node-innovation table keyed by the split connection. Worth knowing before touching crossover.

- `compatibility-body-term-unbounded` — **The morphology term in the compatibility distance is unnormalised and can dominate the topology terms.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_genome/src/lib.rs:688-700` (`compatibility_distance`).
  - Excess and disjoint are divided by gene count and the weight term is an average, so the three NEAT terms are each roughly 0..1, but `body_diff × 0.5` sums nine raw trait deltas (body size range 0.3..2.0, speed 0.2..3.0) and can exceed 2 on its own. With the threshold at 1.3, two organisms with identical brains and a 1.5 difference in speed factor are different species. This is the lever the April threshold sweep was tuning without naming it; any future speciation tuning should look at the relative weights here before moving the threshold again.

- `recurrent-edges-read-zero` — **`mutate_add_connection` can create cycles; cyclic edges then read 0 or a same-tick partial value depending on sort order.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_genome/src/lib.rs:495-523` (no cycle check); `crates/clauvolution_brain/src/lib.rs:178-184` (`topological_sort`, cycle members appended in `all_ids` order); `:83` (`evaluate_trace`, fresh `values` map per call).
  - Kahn's algorithm leaves cycle members unordered; they are appended in neuron-declaration order. Because activations are not carried across ticks, a back-edge contributes either 0 (source not yet evaluated this tick) or the source's fresh value, decided by declaration order, which mutation can change. Not wrong so much as arbitrary: it is a form of recurrence the genome cannot control. Either reject cycles at mutation time (the three explicit memory slots already provide recurrence) or carry last-tick values so back-edges have a defined meaning.

- `crossover-single-blend-factor` — **All nine scalar traits are blended with one shared `t`.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_genome/src/lib.rs:637-651` (`crossover`).
  - Offspring lie on the straight line between the parents in trait space rather than mixing traits independently, so sexual reproduction cannot combine parent A's speed with parent B's armor. Probably unintended; a per-trait `t` is a one-line change but would need a tuning glance.

- `attachment-slot-dead-gene` — **`attachment_slot` is randomised, serialised, never mutated, ignored by `BodyPlan`, and used only as a portrait jitter.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_genome/src/lib.rs:155,165,170-186`; `crates/clauvolution_body/src/lib.rs:29-70` (not read); `crates/clauvolution_ui/src/lib.rs:805` (only read).
  - Parts at similar angles overlap exactly in the world view. Either give the slot a meaning in `BodyPlan` and let it mutate, or remove it.

- `body-plan-never-refreshed` — **`BodyPlan` is computed once via `Without<BodyPlan>` and never recomputed.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_body/src/lib.rs:74-82` (`update_body_plans`).
  - Correct today because genomes are immutable after spawn. Any future in-place genome change (debug tooling, epigenetics, live editing) will not show. Recorded so the assumption is visible.

- `species-representative-arbitrary` — **The representative genome for each species is whichever member archetype iteration yields first.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:1179-1186` (`species_classification_system`, `species_reps` built from the first organism seen per id).
  - Classification outcomes depend on entity iteration order, which is one of the documented sources of non-determinism past ~50 ticks. Picking the member closest to the species centroid, or the oldest member, would make classification stable for a given population.

- `ephemeral-session-devnull` — **Ephemeral sessions set `dir = /dev/null`, and the chronicle relies on the resulting file-open failure being swallowed.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_core/src/lib.rs:80-84` (`Session::new_ephemeral`); `crates/clauvolution_phylogeny/src/lib.rs:139` (`WorldChronicle::log`, `if let Ok(file)`).
  - Works, by accident. An `Option<PathBuf>` for the directory would say what is meant.

- `world-plugin-systems-unordered` — **`food_regeneration_system` and `tile_dynamics_system` are added to `FixedUpdate` with no ordering relative to the sim chain.** Design · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_world/src/lib.rs:12`.
  - Bevy may run them before, after, or interleaved with the chain. Food spawned this tick may or may not be visible to `update_food_snapshot` in the same tick. Harmless today, and the `TODO.md` entry `system-ordering-convention` already asks for a rule; this is a concrete instance for it.

### Performance

- `brain-eval-quadratic-lookups` — **Per-organism per-tick brain evaluation does linear `contains` scans and allocates a fresh `HashMap`.** Performance · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_brain/src/lib.rs:83` (`values` map per call); `:97` (`input_ids.contains` inside the eval loop); `:181` (`order.contains` in `topological_sort`, build-time only).
  - The eval loop is the hottest code in the sim (2000 organisms × 30 Hz). Storing the compiled brain as index-based `Vec`s (inputs first, then eval order) removes both the map allocation and the scan. `topological_sort` runs only at birth, so its quadratic tail matters less. Invariant 5.

- `action-food-scan-quadratic` — **Eating scans the whole food snapshot per organism and checks a growing `eaten_food` vec linearly.** Performance · Open · Read.
  - Where: `crates/clauvolution_sim/src/lib.rs:415` (sensing, nearest-food scan over `FoodSnapshot`); `:563-566` (`action_system`, `eaten_food.contains`); `:1088-1092` (`reproduction_system`, `already_mated.contains` and linear `mate_candidates.iter().find`).
  - Food is already inserted into `SpatialHash` (it indexes every `Position`), so both the sensing scan and the eat loop could be radius queries. `eaten_food` and `already_mated` want a `HashSet`; `mate_candidates` wants a `HashMap<Entity, _>`. At ~26k food items and 2000 organisms the sensing scan alone is ~50M distance checks per tick.

- `chronicle-entries-unbounded` — **`WorldChronicle.entries` grows without bound and the Chronicle tab renders every entry each frame.** Performance · Open · Read.
