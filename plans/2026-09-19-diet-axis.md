# Plan: the diet axis (simulation rules, phase 1)

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** paused after step 3's sweeps. Step 1 landed in #18, step 2 in #19; step 3 (#20) turned every knob below and found that none of them bounds plant growth, so the tuning cannot reach the done-when. It resumes as the last step of `plans/2026-09-20-plant-physics.md`, which adds the two physical couplings that were missing. Step 4 follows it.
**Scope:** phase 1 of `docs/design/simulation-rules.md`. One new dynamic, instrumented first, then tuned and audited. Not phase 2, not the crate layout.

## Why now

Phase 0 shipped its four foundations on 2026-09-18 (#12 to #16) and two of its done-when conditions were not met, for the same reason. With the population cap removed, every audit seed ran to 6000 plants within 800 ticks because nothing consumes a plant; the cap is the carrying capacity. And the founding boom turned out to be driven by food regeneration, not founder energy. Both are waiting on plants having a consumer.

The design doc's reading of the audit is that the food web has no herbivore level. Foragers eat terrain-spawned food items, predators can kill anything, and a plant is the easiest prey there is, so no strategy needs any other strategy to exist. The diet axis is the design doc's answer: digestion becomes a specialisation, an attack on a plant becomes a graze that leaves the plant alive, and a grazer population can persist on a plant population.

Nothing in the design doc's "decide before starting" list blocks this phase. The one phase 1 question, what a specialist keeps at the energy clamp (`energy-clamp-waste`), is assigned to the tuning pass. The performance items named as prerequisites are prerequisites for the ceiling raise and for continents, which come after this phase; phase 1 itself runs under the 2000 ceiling.

The review backlog's three P2 items are independent of this work and stay where they are.

## The mechanism, restated

From `docs/design/simulation-rules.md`, phase 1:

- The genome gains `diet` in -1..1, a scalar trait like the other nine. Plant digestion efficiency is `((1 - diet) / 2)^2`, animal digestion efficiency is `((1 + diet) / 2)^2`. A specialist at either end digests one kind fully; a generalist at zero digests a quarter of each.
- An attack on a photosynthesiser becomes a graze. The grazer takes a bite, a fixed fraction of the plant's energy, keeps the bite times its plant efficiency, and the plant lives on with less.
- An attack on anything else stays a kill. The existing 10% transfer is scaled by the attacker's animal efficiency.
- Terrain food items count as plant tissue. Their regeneration is reduced so living plants become the primary food; whether they go entirely is decided in the tuning pass.
- Strategy classification becomes plant, grazer, hunter, and omnivore, from photosynthesis rate and diet.
- Brain inputs do not change.

## The plan

Four steps, in order. Steps 1 to 3 are each one branch and one pull request; step 4 is a measurement that may produce a small pull request. The project's rule for a new dynamic is instrument first, then the dynamic, then tune, and the steps follow that order. Each step's pull request carries its own `docs/DECISIONS.md` entries, runs `scripts/check.sh`, and updates `docs/FEATURES.md` where the sim's visible behaviour changes.

### 1. The trait and the instruments, with no behaviour change

Add everything that lets the diet axis be seen, without any system yet acting on it.

- **Genome.** `diet: f32` with `DIET_BOUNDS = (-1.0, 1.0)`, added to `SCALAR_TRAIT_BOUNDS`, `scalar_traits()`, `mutate`, per-trait crossover, and the save format with `#[serde(default)]` so existing saves load. Founders initialise it the way the other scalar traits are initialised (a narrow band around neutral; see open questions). `Genome` gets `plant_efficiency()` and `animal_efficiency()` so the curve lives in one place. Adding a trait changes `compatibility_distance`, so the species threshold is re-swept per the phase 0 finding (1.1 was a cliff at nine traits) and the new cell recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- **Predicate.** Claim `photosynthesiser-predicate` from `TODO.md`: one `Genome::is_photosynthesiser()` replaces the remaining copies of the "is this a plant" rule. Step 2 needs exactly this predicate at the graze branch, so it belongs here.
- **Classification.** `SpeciesStrategy` becomes `Photosynthesizer`, `Grazer`, `Hunter`, `Omnivore`. `classify_strategy` keeps the plant rule first, then splits the rest by `diet` around a named threshold. The save format's strategy index gains a fourth value; loaded saves reclassify within one classification period anyway.
- **Population history and outputs.** `PopSnapshot` replaces `foragers` and `predators` with `grazers`, `hunters`, `omnivores`, and adds `avg_diet`. The `--dump-history` CSV header, the headless summary block, `scripts/attractor_audit_summary.py`, the Graphs strategy plot, the header and Phylo tab labels and icons, and `strategy_noun` in species naming all follow. Inspect shows the organism's diet and both efficiencies.
- **Ledger.** `EnergyFlows` gains `grazing` (energy credited to grazers) and `digestion` (energy lost to inefficient digestion). Both stay zero in this step. The Graphs flows chart and the headless ledger block show them.

Done when: a 5000-tick headless run on seeds 1, 3 and 42 has the same plant count and total population as main at the same tick to within same-seed noise, the new columns and series are populated, the ledger residual is still zero to rounding, and `docs/DECISIONS.md` records the re-swept threshold and the diet distribution after 5000 ticks of neutral drift. That drift number is the baseline step 2 is measured against.

### 2. Grazing and digestion

Make the systems read the trait.

- **Grazing.** In `predation_system`, when a target passes the existing attack gates and `is_photosynthesiser()`, the attack is a graze rather than a kill. The bite is `BITE_FRACTION` of the plant's current energy. The plant's energy drops by the bite; the grazer is credited the bite times its plant efficiency through the existing clamp path; the undigested share goes to `digestion`. The plant is not marked `Killed` and is not claimed for the tick, so it can be grazed again by another grazer only if the design decides so (see open questions). The action flash distinguishes a graze from an attack.
- **Kills.** For non-plant targets the transfer becomes `victim_energy * 0.1 * animal_efficiency`; the remainder goes to `death` as now.
- **Food items.** Eating a food item credits `food_energy_value * plant_efficiency`; the rest goes to `digestion`.
- **Attribution.** A plant that a bite takes to zero energy dies in `death_system` as it would from any other energy loss. Whether that reads as starvation or needs its own cause is settled in the pull request.
- **No tuning yet.** `BITE_FRACTION` starts small, on the order of a tenth, because the stated risk is plants going extinct before grazers specialise. The exponent stays at 2. Food regeneration stays where it is. The point of this step is to see the flows, not to balance them.

Done when: on seeds 1, 3 and 42 at 5000 ticks, the `grazing` flow is non-zero, the ledger residual is still zero to rounding, `avg_diet` has moved away from the step 1 drift baseline on at least one seed in a direction the flows explain, and `docs/DECISIONS.md` records the mechanism and the opening constants. Plants may crash in this step; that is information for step 3, not a failure.

**Step 3 outcome (2026-09-19):** founder spread 1.0 puts the axis under selection from tick 0 (grazer peaks of 500 to 1600 by tick 300), and then no knob keeps the consumers alive: bite fraction, energy cap, kill share, food items, fair birth admission and the ceiling level all end with a handful of grazers and no hunters at 5000 ticks. With no ceiling, plants doubled every 50 ticks from 2.3k to 98k while the grazers were still growing. Photosynthesis is free, universal and twelve times a plant's upkeep, per-tile shading never engages, and plants can move as freely as anything else. Sweep tables and the reading are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Diet axis tuning pass".

**Step 2 outcome (2026-09-19):** grazing flow non-zero and residual at rounding, met; diet movement, not met. The consumers crashed rather than the plants: non-plants fell to 2, 8 and 20 organisms by 5000 ticks while plants filled the cap and the clamp destroyed about two thirds of photosynthesis income. Digestion cut food-item income to a quarter at once, and bites were rare (the damage gate needs claws founders lack) and small (a generalist keeps 3 energy of a 12-energy bite). Selection had no consumers left to act on. Full numbers in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Grazing". Step 3 starts from three levers in this order: the founder diet band (open question 1, now triggered), bite reach and size, and the clamp.

### 3. The tuning pass and the audit

Turn the knobs until the design doc's done-when holds, then measure.

- **Knobs.** The founder diet band, `BITE_FRACTION` and the graze gate, the efficiency exponent, `max_food_density` and `food_regen_rate` (which also owns `founding-boom-food-regen`), and whether terrain food items survive at all. Watched through the ledger flows and the four-way strategy plot. Step 2's outcome says the first three matter most: consumers must survive the arrival of digestion long enough to be selected on.
- **The clamp.** Decide `energy-clamp-waste` here: a higher cap, a body-scaled cap, or excess feeding reproduction readiness. The diet axis makes what a specialist keeps from a meal part of the same question, and the ledger's `clamp` flow is the measurement.
- **The interdependence test.** A headless override, `--animal-efficiency <f>`, multiplies every attacker's animal efficiency, default 1.0. At 0 nobody can live by hunting. The claim to check is that grazers then overrun plants and crash. Its numbers go in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- **The audit.** `scripts/attractor_audit.sh` on the eight seeds, two runs each, 15k ticks, on the merged commit. A dated block in the roadmap's attractor-states section beside the 2026-09-18 one, and a "Phase 1 outcome" section in the design doc modelled on the phase 0 one.
- **Threshold.** Re-sweep the species threshold once after tuning, since selection on `diet` changes the trait's spread.

Done when: on the audit seeds at 15k ticks, plants, grazers and hunters all persist on most seeds; the interdependence test shows the grazer overshoot and crash; the roadmap and design doc have their dated blocks; and every constant chosen has a `docs/DECISIONS.md` entry with the alternatives tried.

### 4. Measure the plateau under a raised ceiling

Phase 0 sequenced the ceiling raise after grazing. This step finds out whether the raise is now possible; it does not make it.

- Add `--population-ceiling <n>` as a headless override in the style of `--species-threshold`.
- Run three audit seeds at 15k ticks with the ceiling at 6000. Record whether the population plateaus below the ceiling, at what level, whether the ledger's income flows account for it, and the tick cost relative to the 2000 runs (`reproduction-linear-scans`, `moisture-fix-tick-cost`).

Done when: the numbers are in the design doc's phase 1 outcome section with a recommendation: raise the default now, raise it after the named performance items, or leave it. The raise itself, if recommended, is its own pull request outside this plan.

## Open questions

- **Founder diet.** The other scalar traits start in a narrow band around neutral (armor 0..0.1, symbiosis -0.2..0.2), which for `diet` means every founder is an omnivore at a quarter efficiency on both kinds of food. That is a hard opening and it is the convention. Recommendation: follow the convention in step 1, and if step 2 shows nothing escaping the middle, widen the founder band before touching the exponent.
- **Do grazes keep the attack gates?** Today an attack needs the attack output above 0.5, attacker size above 0.6 of the target's, and claw strength minus half the target's armour above 0.1. Keeping the gates means grazers need claws, which cost quadratically, and plant armour already defends against grazing, which is the hook plant defences will hang on. Dropping the size gate would let small grazers bite big plants, which reads more like grazing. Recommendation: keep the damage gate, drop the size gate for grazes, and record it.
- **One bite per plant per tick, or many?** Claiming the plant for the tick, as kills do, caps grazing pressure per plant at one bite per tick. Not claiming it lets a herd strip a plant in one tick. Recommendation: claim it in step 2, revisit in step 3 if grazers starve beside full plants.
- **Terrain food items.** Reduce, or remove? The design doc leaves it to the tuning pass. The founding boom lives here too.
- **Where the digestion loss shows.** As its own line in the Graphs flows chart, or folded into a "losses" group with `clamp` and `death`. Small enough to settle in step 1.
